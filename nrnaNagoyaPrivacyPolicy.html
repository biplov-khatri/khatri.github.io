Privacy Policy for the official NRNA Nagoya mobile application.

Last updated: August 27, 2026
This Privacy Policy explains how the NRNA Nagoya mobile application handles information and how responsibility for the application's content and technical services is divided between NRNA Nagoya and Khatori Tech.

1. About the NRNA Nagoya App

NRNA Nagoya is an information and communication application operated for the NRNA Nagoya institution. The application provides users with news, announcements, updates, events, and other information published by NRNA Nagoya.

The application is intended to make information from NRNA Nagoya easily accessible to users through a mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

The NRNA Nagoya application does not collect or require users to provide personal information.

Account No account or registration is required.
Name & Email We do not request or collect your name or email address.
Phone Number We do not request or collect phone numbers.
Personal Data No personal information is intentionally collected from users through the application.

Users can access the application's information without creating an account or submitting personal information.

3. Information and Content Provided by NRNA Nagoya

The application displays information, news, announcements, events, and other content provided or published by NRNA Nagoya.

Such content is the responsibility of NRNA Nagoya. Khatori Tech provides technical development and hosting services for the application and does not create, control, or determine the institutional content published by NRNA Nagoya.

4. Push Notifications

The application may send push notifications when new news, announcements, or other information is published or updated by NRNA Nagoya.

These notifications are intended to keep users informed about newly available information. Users may manage notification permissions through their device settings.

5. Data Storage

The NRNA Nagoya application does not provide a service for users to upload or store personal information.

No user profile, personal account, personal documents, photographs, names, email addresses, or other personal information are intentionally hosted as part of the application's user functionality.

6. Third-Party Services

The application may use technical services necessary to deliver application functionality, including services required for push notifications and delivery of application content.

These services may process technical information necessary for the operation of the service, such as device or network-related information, according to their respective policies.

The application does not intentionally use these services to collect personal information from users for its own purposes.

7. Responsibility for Application Content

NRNA Nagoya

NRNA Nagoya is responsible for the institutional information, news, announcements, events, notices, and other content published through the application.

Khatori Tech

Khatori Tech provides technical development, maintenance, and hosting services for the application. Khatori Tech is not responsible for the institutional content published or provided by NRNA Nagoya.

8. Children's Privacy

The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Since the application does not require users to create accounts or submit personal information, there is no intentional collection of children's personal data through the application.

9. Security

Appropriate technical measures are used to maintain the application and protect the services used to provide its functionality.

However, no method of electronic transmission or internet-based service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the application, its services, or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page together with a revised "Last updated" date.
